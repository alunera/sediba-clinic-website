name: Sedi service catalog
description: Defines which treatment catalog the AI concierge should treat as authoritative.
---

Sedi must quote the branded treatment names and prices shown on the public Services page, never a separately maintained assistant catalogue.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected the Services-page menu when the two catalogs were found to differ. Clients should hear the same names and prices from Sedi that they see publicly.

**How to apply:** Keep Sedi's service answers aligned with the shared public branded menu. Preserve public-menu naming and pricing when reconciling booking data.

Sedi's conversational booking stops at a validated handoff to the existing booking form; client details, policy acceptance and payment remain in that form.

**Why:** The user explicitly prohibited a second booking, availability or payment system. Reusing the established form avoids collecting booking personal data in chat and preserves the existing confirmation rules.

**How to apply:** Extend read-only conversational assistance as needed, but do not add chat-driven appointment creation or payment processing without revisiting this boundary.
